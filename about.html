About School

About Prestige Senior Secondary School

Established in 1985, Prestige Senior Secondary School has been a beacon of academic excellence and holistic development. With decades of service to education, the school fosters curiosity, leadership, and community responsibility among its students.

Our Mission

To empower students through knowledge, character, and discipline—preparing them to face the challenges of life and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be a model institution of learning that integrates innovation, values, and global competence, while nurturing a passion for excellence.

Our History

From a humble beginning, the school has grown into a reputed institution offering quality education to thousands of students. It has evolved with time, adapting modern teaching methodologies and maintaining high academic standards.

Our Leadership

Dr. Evelyn Reed - Principal

Dr. Reed brings decades of academic leadership and a passion for innovative education that builds student confidence and competency.

Mr. Jonathan Blake - Director

With a strategic vision for growth, Mr. Blake oversees the development of infrastructure and institutional goals to support learning at all levels.

General Instructions

  • Students must maintain discipline and respect school rules at all times.
  • Regular attendance is mandatory; prior notice required for planned absences.
  • Students must wear the prescribed uniform neatly and with pride.
  • Use of mobile phones and gadgets is restricted unless approved for educational use.
  • Respect for staff, peers, and property is essential to school harmony.
  • Parents are expected to attend all scheduled meetings and review academic updates.
