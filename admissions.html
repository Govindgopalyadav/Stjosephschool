Admissions

Admission Process

  1. Collect the application form from the school office or download online.
  2. Submit the completed form with necessary documents.
  3. Attend entrance test/interview (if applicable).
  4. Wait for merit list and confirmation notice.
  5. Complete admission formalities and fee payment.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Successful completion of Class X from a recognized board.
  • Minimum marks as prescribed for Science/Commerce/Humanities streams.
  • Performance in entrance or interaction (if applicable).

Documents Required

  • Marksheet and Transfer Certificate (original & copy)
  • Photographs of student and parents
  • Address and ID proof
  • Medical certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport-size photos (4 copies)

Important Dates

  • Application Open: March 1st
  • Last Date: April 15th
  • Entrance Test: April 25th
  • Results Announcement: May 5th

(Dates are tentative and subject to updates.)
