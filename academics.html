Academics

Curriculum Overview

Our academic program is designed in accordance with the CBSE curriculum and emphasizes holistic development. It integrates knowledge, practical application, creativity, and values—preparing students for higher education and real-world success.

Co-Curricular Activities

At Prestige Senior Secondary School, we strongly believe that education goes beyond the classroom. Our co-curricular programs are designed to nurture talent, teamwork, leadership, and creativity in our students.

Student Clubs

Debate Club, Science Club, Literary Society, Eco Club, Photography Club, etc.

Performing & Visual Arts

Regular training in dance, music, theatre, painting, and crafts.

Sports & Athletics

Inter-house competitions, football, cricket, yoga, martial arts, and more.

Leadership & Community

Student council, school ambassador program, and social outreach events.

Workshops & Guest Lectures

Skill-building sessions, motivational speakers, and expert-led training.

Streams Offered (Senior Secondary)

  • Science: English, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology / Computer Science
  • Commerce: English, Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics / Informatics Practices
  • Humanities: English, History, Political Science, Geography, Sociology / Psychology / Economics

* Optional and vocational subjects are available based on interest and availability.

Departments

Science Mathematics Commerce Humanities English Computer Science Physical Education Art & Music

Examination & Assessment

We follow a Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) model that includes:

  • Formative assessments (quizzes, assignments, oral tests)
  • Summative examinations (mid-terms, finals)
  • Practical examinations for Science and Computer subjects
  • Project work and internal assessments
